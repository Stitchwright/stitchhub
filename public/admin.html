Accounts

Shop owners' StitchHub accounts (/account): email, whether the address is confirmed, and the shops each one owns. "Resend" mails the set-password or confirmation link again. "Delete" is for an owner who asks: the account goes, its shops stay on the hub with no account. "Founding shop" gives the account the founding prices ($15 Solo, $30 Shop a month; $150 / $300 a year) when it subscribes or switches: the first 25 only, kept while its subscription runs without a gap of more than 60 days (after that the mark goes by itself).

Shops

Every company that has told the hub about itself, with its standing: a 30-day trial from its first sync, a subscription through Stripe (Solo, up to 2 people signed in, $25 a month or $250 a year; Shop, everyone, $45 or $450; founding prices for founding accounts), free while partnered with a repair shop in good standing (a sewing or quilt shop), or free with your compliments ("Comp": a last free day, or for good). "Lapse now" ends a shop's standing at once (the program is read-only from its next sync); a comp, a payment or "Undo lapse" takes it back. "Open in Stripe" shows the owner's customer, or the subscription, in your Stripe dashboard.

Moves

Companies moving from one install to another (Settings → Company → Move this company, in StitchBench). The program the company leaves gets a move token for the move file; the install it moves to redeems it, and the company's partner links, listings, orders, ratings, notes and parts usage follow it. Partner shops see nothing change. "Move this shop" does the same by hand, onto an install already on the hub: for a move file made while the hub was down, or one whose token expired (14 days).

Manual repository

Files shops offered, waiting for your review. The checklist: a manual, readable and whole; brand, models, document type and year right (correct them below); nothing private in it; the automatic checks passed; not a duplicate of a better file; not a work an earlier notice named; not marked confidential, dealer-only or not for distribution (the markings flag below: a hit shows the page and the words around it, and is for you to judge; "checking" means a scan is being read by OCR (reload in a minute); "not checked" means OCR failed, timed out or is off, or the text was unreadable, so look at the cover yourself). Preview opens the file for five minutes.

Copyright notices

A notice that is complete (signature, the work, the material and where it is, contact details, the good-faith and accuracy statements) and names a manual's reference or file hash is acted on at once. Others wait here: match the manual and press Action within one business day. Action removes the manual from the feed and the free page, holds the file, blocks its hash, gives the uploader a strike and mails them. When the uploader counter-notices, the complainant is mailed a copy; press Restore from business day 10 unless the complainant has said it filed in court. Download the log (CSV).

Figure snapshots

A wrong or unwanted figure can be taken down: installs drop their copy, and any shop can share that figure again. Its id is the end of the picture's address in StitchBench (right-click the figure, Copy image address). To take down every figure one shop shared, use Remove figures under Members.

Data sets

Files every install downloads when they change, e.g. specs (factory tension and timing values, made by StitchBench's scripts/hub/publish-specs).

Moving the hub

Export everything (members, join key, links with history, notes, photos, figures, data sets) to one file; import it on a new StitchHub server. Keep an export somewhere safe now and then.

Add links in bulk

A JSON array of links (brand, models, docType, title, url, fileName, category). Links already on the hub are skipped.

Hub

Join key

Email this to members (for example after they pay). Changing it stops new people joining with the old one; shops already joined keep working.

Member invite

One invite holds this hub's address, the join key and your Discord link. Pin it in your members-only Discord channel or email it. In StitchBench, members paste the code (or open the file) under Settings → Community hub.

Discord

Invite link: goes into the member invite, and shows in StitchBench as "Community chat".

Channel webhook (Discord → channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks): the hub posts new notes, link fixes and new members there.

Figure snapshots

A wrong or unwanted figure can be taken down: installs drop their copy, and any shop can share that figure again. Its id is the end of the picture's address in StitchBench (right-click the figure, Copy image address). To take down every figure one shop shared, use Remove figures under Members.

Data sets

Files every install downloads when they change, e.g. specs (factory tension and timing values, made by StitchBench's scripts/hub/publish-specs).

Moving the hub

Export everything (members, join key, links with history, notes, photos, figures, data sets) to one file; import it on a new StitchHub server. Keep an export somewhere safe now and then.

Add links in bulk

A JSON array of links (brand, models, docType, title, url, fileName, category). Links already on the hub are skipped.
